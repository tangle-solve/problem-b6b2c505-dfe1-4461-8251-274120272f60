### ALGO-000157: Polynomial-bit separation for sums of square roots
- **Subfield:** Computational geometry
- **Open:** Prove an inverse-exponential lower bound \(2^{-\mathrm{poly}(k,\log N)}\) on the absolute value of any nonzero signed sum of \(k\) square roots of integers at most \(N\).
- **Status:** Known general separation bounds can require much more precision. The proposed polynomial-bit bound would settle important exact-comparison questions.
- **Type:** theory
- **Why:** Converts exact symbolic comparison into polynomial-precision numerical computation.
- **Key works:** Burnikel, Fleischer, Mehlhorn, and Schirra (2000), radical separation bounds; Cheng, Meng, Sun, and Chen (2009), *Bounding Sum of Square Roots via Lattice Reduction*, arXiv:0905.4487.
- **Related:** Number theory—Diophantine approximation.
